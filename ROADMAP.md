# Roadmap

Milestones are unlocked by tokens sold, not by dates. Everything here is public on the site as it happens.

| Tokens sold | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 | The first memory. The Brain stops being empty. |
| 1,000 memories | The founder places are gone. Founders keep their gold star and their place at the core forever. |
| 10,000 | The Brain learns Spanish: the site opens in Spanish. |
| 50,000 | First $5,000 micro-grant unlocks. Applications are open from day one. |
| 100,000 | First letter to the internet. The Ideas board opens: what should the Brain's next life be? |
| every 100,000 | A new letter. |
| every 50,000 | A new micro-grant, chosen with a public vote and a public interview of the finalists by the Brain. |
| 500,000 | Half full. The Brain writes its mid-life letter. |
| 1,000,000 | Graduation. The memory freezes. Code and memory are released here as open source. The life fund keeps the Brain talking. |
| + 3 years | Whatever is left in the life fund goes to the charities. |

## After graduation

Decided by the Ideas board and the token holders: a version 2.0, a new brain, or a very quiet retirement. If it happens, it will be because people asked for it.

## Things we are building next

- A weekly picture of the Wall, posted automatically.
- Shareable cards for teachers ("I'm #1 today") and their profiles.
- The Brain's gifts: small games and surprises for people who talk to it.
- Public debates between memories and a trivia mode.

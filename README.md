# The Million Dollar Brain

**A public AI that only knows what people paid it to know.**

One brain. One million tokens of memory for sale, one dollar per token, permanent. Anyone can chat with it for free, and it answers only from what its sponsors taught it. When the millionth token is sold, its memory freezes forever.

🧠 **Site:** https://themilliondollarbrain.com · 🧱 **The Wall:** https://themilliondollarbrain.com/memory?view=wall · 📖 **Blog:** https://themilliondollarbrain.com/blog

## Why this repo exists

- **The Brain's memory, as a public dataset.** [`memory/llms.txt`](memory/llms.txt) is a copy of everything the Brain knows, refreshed every day by a GitHub Action. Every memory ever bought stays in this repo's history. That is the permanence promise, verifiable by anyone.
- **The graduation promise.** At one million tokens, the code and the complete memory of the Brain are released here as open source. Star the repo and you will be here when it happens.
- **A place to talk.** Bugs, ideas and questions go in [Issues](../../issues). The Brain's own wish list lives on the [Ideas board](https://themilliondollarbrain.com/ideas).

## A homage

In 2005 a student sold a million pixels for a dollar each and built [The Million Dollar Homepage](https://milliondollarhomepage.com/). Twenty years later we made the same bet with the thing that defines this decade: a brain instead of a billboard. A pixel just sits there. A memory inside an AI gets repeated, argued about, recommended and misquoted by everyone who talks to it. We are not affiliated with the original. We are a fan letter to it.

## Where the money goes

Thirty cents of every dollar go back, measured on revenue excluding VAT:

| Share | Goes to |
|---|---|
| 10 ¢ | Three charities: [Fundación Pasqual Maragall](https://fpmaragall.org/) (Alzheimer's research), [World Central Kitchen](https://wck.org/), [Code.org](https://code.org/) |
| 10 ¢ | Micro-grants of $5,000 for independent AI projects with a social purpose, one per $50,000 sold, chosen with a public vote |
| 10 ¢ | The Brain's life fund, which pays its AI bill after it is full. Whatever is left three years after graduation goes to the charities |

The other 70 cents keep the Brain alive and moving: the AI and hosting bill, payment fees and taxes, the people who build and moderate it, and what is left funds related AI projects and the Brain's version 2.0. Receipts, the AI bill and the fund balance are published on the [Transparency page](https://themilliondollarbrain.com/transparency).

## How it works, in short

- **Teach it**: write a memory, pay $1 per token, it is permanent. One link per memory, an emoji for everyone, a logo from 100 tokens.
- **Founders**: the first thousand memories sit at the core of its neural network and it always keeps some of them in mind.
- **It actually talks**: thousands of free chats a day. When your topic comes up, the Brain remembers you and shares your link.
- **Letters**: every 100,000 tokens the Brain writes an open letter to the internet.
- **The Wall**: one million squares, one tile per memory, sized by its tokens, placed in order of purchase, forever.
- **Rules**: no adult content, hate, scams or attempts to give the Brain orders. It ignores orders hidden in memories and, if asked, tells on them.
